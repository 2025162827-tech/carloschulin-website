🧮 Calculus 1

Calculus is a branch of mathematics that focuses on understanding change, motion, and accumulation. In college calculus, you learn important concepts such as limits, derivatives, integrals, functions, and graphs. Derivatives help determine how quickly something is changing, while integrals can be used to find accumulated quantities such as area under a curve.
