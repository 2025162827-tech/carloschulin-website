💻 Fundamentals of Computing

Fundamentals of Computing is the study of the basic concepts and principles of computers and technology. It includes learning about computer hardware, software, operating systems, networks, data, and how computers process and store information. It also introduces basic programming, problem-solving, and the use of technology to complete different tasks. Understanding the fundamentals of computing provides a strong foundation for learning more advanced computer skills and technologies.
