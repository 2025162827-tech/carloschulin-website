About College English 2

📝 College English 2

College English 2 is a course that builds your skills in academic writing, reading, research, and communication. You learn how to develop and organize ideas, write stronger essays and arguments, use rhetorical appeals such as ethos, pathos, and logos, evaluate sources, avoid plagiarism, and use APA style when required. The course is mainly about communicating your ideas clearly and supporting them with reliable evidence.
